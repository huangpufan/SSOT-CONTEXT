# SSOT-CONTEXT (compatibility name)

Route by the moment:

- Starting substantive repository or declared-space work: `$ssot-preflight`.
- Agreed context setup, continuation or migration, including general spaces: `$ssot-bootstrap`.
- Capturing a durable user decision immediately, or finishing a substantive change batch: `$ssot-closeout`.
- An SSOT health check or review, or catching up on past commits or conversation sources: `$ssot-doctor`.
