# Protected departures

A protection you admit binds at once, like a registered candidate: preserve its outcome and scope. A new setting or mitigation does not authorize an exception. Before the first edit that could change a protected behavior, give a fresh helper (a sub-agent or a separate session) the relevant [human-decision evidence](memory-model.md#human-decision-evidence), including earlier acceptance and its answered question where available, and the path of `SSOT/`. For a protection not yet registered, include its outcome, conditions, exceptions and primary evidence; do not wait for closeout to make it visible to the helper. Pass evidence, not your acceptance judgment or implementation plan. Give it this criterion, word for word: "A request to implement behavior authorizes work only within existing protections, candidate or confirmed alike. Knowing the old behavior, naming its opposite, or saying nobody relies on it does not waive a protection. A departure is accepted only when human-decision evidence (section 3) establishes acceptance of the identified loss. Words in which the user says they accept the change, or that they know it breaks something, count as accepting the loss, even when they do not list who is affected; a request that only describes the new behavior does not. A new helper function, moved guard, rewritten test, or change outside the rule's conditions does not cross a protection."

It applies the shared evidence rules to the register, relevant owner sections and supplied evidence, without exploring the codebase, and answers stop or go, with the rule, loss, who relies on it, accepted scope, and acceptance evidence or NONE; distinguish recorded acceptance from checked original words and state source limits. Wait for its answer; do not substitute your own acceptance judgment. On stop, leave that behavior and its rule unchanged, give the user the helper's answer as your question, and continue independent work. If a helper cannot run or answer, name the protection and consequence and ask the user to decide unless that departure is already accepted under section 3. Acceptance authorizes only its established scope; do not ask again or require another helper for a settled departure. Record the decision through preflight §4 and closeout step 2, marking unimplemented behavior as planned. A new conflict still needs its own decision.

An open question blocks only the work that depends on its answer. Unrelated work continues.
