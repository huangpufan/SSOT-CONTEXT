# Context tiers and changed guidance

## When reading has become costly

Use this section for an oversized entry, repeated reconstruction from history, or an explicit tiering/compression request. Ordinary tasks keep their existing routes. Tiers describe access, not new directories, copies or a universal schema:

| Tier | Purpose | Reading |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hot | A small orientation entry and the current task's working set: purpose, relevant constraints, current direction and blocking boundaries | Read what makes the next action safe and understandable |
| Warm | Maintained topic owners explaining current knowledge, conditions, reasons and important exceptions | Follow task routes to the relevant sections |
| Cold | Historical decisions, original exchanges, completed process and detailed evidence in their existing owners | Retrieve the specific source when tracing reasons, reviewing a decision or resolving a conflict |

Choose by task relevance, likelihood of reuse, cost of omission and cost of reconstruction. Age and access frequency alone do not retire a rule. Keep consequential constraints discoverable before the actions they govern; their full explanation may stay in the linked owner. Access temperature and authority are independent: an old accepted constraint still binds, while a recent rejected proposal does not. Reading a cold record never reactivates it.

**Recognizable cold targets and intra-file layering.** Keep current governing state at the top of active files so partial head-reads land on current truth instead of old logs, and mark cold targets consistently so routine preflight and `ssot-check.py` skip them:

- **File-level cold archives (default for Format 4 and multi-file spaces):** optional YAML frontmatter `ssot_tier: cold`, or path conventions (`decisions/**` excluding directory indexes, concluded `research/`, `archive/**`, `history/**`, `*-history.md`, `*-archive.md`, and cold retired shards such as `invariants/retired.md` or shards with `ssot_tier: cold`). Always prefer offloading superseded history to a separate cold file when file creation is allowed so routine whole-file reads actually shrink.
- **Intra-file trailing cold tail (strictly for fixed single-file layouts or brief footnotes):** a clearly marked section strictly at the bottom of a file—`## History (cold)` / `## Archive (cold)` (or `## 历史记录（冷读）` / `## 历史归档（冷读）`), or `<!-- ssot:cold -->` before a custom heading. Reserve this for single-file logs where splitting out new files is not yet authorized (such as a Format-5 chronological `decisions.md`)—never use an inline cold tail in a Format-4 warm doc as a substitute for offloading multi-KB history to `decisions/` or `<topic>-history.md`. In a fixed single-file log, consolidating current governing rules at the top and pushing superseded chronological entries below this trailing heading gives zero unauthorized file creation and 100% lossless history.

When a task needs cold material, follow a topic/source locator and read the relevant question, answer, conditions and later replacements together. Missing explanations or conflicting owners justify this expansion; do not forbid it to meet a reading budget. Inaccessible sources remain a disclosed gap. Reuse unchanged passages under preflight's freshness rule. The skill guides selection and persistence; the host controls eviction of material already in the model context and any physical storage/cache tiers.

## Consolidate without losing the way back

Within the authorised scope, replace repeated process in hot entries with a readable current explanation and routes. When an oversized entry is driven by a growing inline invariants register, prefer [optional topic shards](memory-model.md#5-invariants) and a cold retired shard so daily entry reads stay small without losing protection checks. Consolidate warm knowledge in its existing owner by semantic topic—never lossy summarization that drops exceptions or demotion by age—avoid a second full-topic summary, and avoid routing routine startup to an unbounded chronological log when current-state owners exist. A short derived statement needs its owner link and changes with it. Keep [cold indexes](memory-layout.md#decisions) to one-line locators without replaying summaries.

### Compaction boundaries

- **Auto-compact in place (touched authorized SSOT-owned files/rows):** merge superseded guidance and chronological logs into present-tense current state; **delete single-task verification/test-run receipts** (commits and CI own them—do not archive them into cold history); offload superseded clauses, rejected trials, and lengthy quotes to a separate cold file (`decisions/` or `<topic>-history.md`) by default—reserving trailing `## History (cold)` strictly for fixed single-file layouts; remove verified resolved ephemera (resolved `Open questions` / `Known gaps`, completed `- [x]` entry checklist items, shipped `"Planned"` labels, fixed `gotchas.md` traps, met-condition workarounds, and closed Entry task links); trim bloated `Source` cells (`>500` chars, keeping `ID`/`Kind`/`Rule`/`Status` exact on `confirmed` rows); move newly retired rows to an existing cold retired shard; keep cold-index rows to one-line locators (collapsing summary replays in a touched index).
- **Propose in one line without reading untouched files (do not do silently during routine closeout):** first-time layout splits (first-time invariant sharding or splitting a `>16,000`-character warm doc into a folder), untouched pre-existing bloat, Format-5 multi-file layout splits, external canonical owners, tightening a `confirmed` invariant's `Rule` wording, deleting files, or removing workarounds whose removal condition is unknown or only proposed. Never open untouched files, external owners, `memory-layout.md`, or `context-tiering.md` solely because `ssot-check.py` flagged these—relay the concrete target and action directly from the checker summary line and stop. Inside an authorized `$ssot-doctor` repair or `$ssot-bootstrap`, SSOT-owned layout splits proceed within requested scope while external, Format-5 user-owned, and `confirmed`-rule changes still need user confirmation.
- **Never do:** age-based demotion of active rules, lossy summarization dropping exceptions/conditions/rejection reasons/attribution, editing frozen/unauthorized/v2/v3/unknown files, opening withdrawn or restricted sources without clearance, deleting a `retired` invariant row, or editing instruction files outside managed markers.

Before moving, compressing or removing content, use the [preservation check](memory-layout.md#preserving-content-when-reorganizing). Account for distinct constraints, exceptions, rejection reasons, uncertainty and source words, and keep `<!-- ssot-source:... -->` markers intact unless reconciling through `context-source.py`. Preserve authorised originals in their existing owner; summaries do not replace them. Historical retention remains subject to the space's withdrawal/deletion authority. File splitting and byte compression alone do not prove reduced reading cost. Do not create access counters, a migration ledger or a mandatory three-folder layout.

## Reconcile changed guidance

Trace a durable replacement through the authorised routes, topic owners, instructions, summaries, templates and examples that use it. Search for the old behaviour as well as its names and values. If a goal or method changes, recheck dependent steps and what their evidence establishes. Update task records you control. For another executor, update the guidance it consults and report a missing route. That executor reconciles in-flight effects. For a possible conflict with a protection, follow [departure review](protection-departure.md).

Before replacing a passage, locate the earlier choice and its stated reason. Link another retained, usable source when available. If this passage alone carries the history, preserve its necessary original words, attribution, and reason within current retention permission; when superseding guidance or consolidating prior revisions on a topic, move superseded wording and lengthy verbatim quotes to a separate [cold archive](#when-reading-has-become-costly) (`decisions/` or `<topic>-history.md`) with a one-line link—using a trailing `## History (cold)` heading only when single-file layout is fixed—and delete single-task verification receipts rather than archiving them. Leave a missing reason unknown. Mark exactly which clause its successor replaces and when. Keep unaffected terms, exceptions and their standing. Update current uses and moved links so readers find what applies during the transition and afterwards.

For a frozen or unauthorised dependent owner, leave its content unchanged. In the nearest authorised route or existing follow-up owner, identify the affected topic or obsolete concept, the current owner section and the established reopening condition (or say it is unknown). Make the affected part recognisable without copying the entire replacement. Replace obsolete intermediate follow-ups rather than accumulating another full copy of the target facts. Treat the affected content as pending, not current; unrelated valid content and independent work remain usable. Unlocking requires the applicable authority; once granted, refresh from the current owner, reconcile the pending uses and clear only completed deferrals.

Before calling this change reconciled, read back the changed owner, affected live uses, historical replacement notices and any pending boundary. Report unresolved affected uses; keyword or link checks cannot establish semantic agreement. For a tiering change, also try a representative task from the entry and a targeted historical question. Compare whole-task reading, retrieval, writing and mistakes; count maintenance cost and preserve important exceptions. Without observed task evidence, claim only the structural changes checked.
