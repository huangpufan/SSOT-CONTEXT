# Context tiers and changed guidance

## When reading has become costly

Use this section for an oversized entry, repeated reconstruction from history, or an explicit tiering/compression request. Ordinary tasks keep their existing routes. Tiers describe access, not new directories, copies or a universal schema:

| Tier | Purpose | Reading |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hot | A small orientation entry and the current task's working set: purpose, relevant constraints, current direction and blocking boundaries | Read what makes the next action safe and understandable |
| Warm | Maintained topic owners explaining current knowledge, conditions, reasons and important exceptions | Follow task routes to the relevant sections |
| Cold | Historical decisions, original exchanges, completed process and detailed evidence in their existing owners | Retrieve the specific source when tracing reasons, reviewing a decision or resolving a conflict |

Choose by task relevance, likelihood of reuse, cost of omission and cost of reconstruction. Age and access frequency alone do not retire a rule. Keep consequential constraints discoverable before the actions they govern; their full explanation may stay in the linked owner. Access temperature and authority are independent: an old accepted constraint still binds, while a recent rejected proposal does not. Reading a cold record never reactivates it.

When a task needs cold material, follow a topic/source locator and read the relevant question, answer, conditions and later replacements together. Missing explanations or conflicting owners justify this expansion; do not forbid it to meet a reading budget. Inaccessible sources remain a disclosed gap. Reuse unchanged passages under preflight's freshness rule. The skill guides selection and persistence; the host controls eviction of material already in the model context and any physical storage/cache tiers.

## Consolidate without losing the way back

Within the authorised scope, replace repeated process in hot entries with a readable current explanation and routes. Consolidate warm knowledge in its existing owner; avoid making a second manually maintained summary of the whole topic. A short derived statement needs its owner link and changes with that owner. A cold index should make a reason findable without replaying the archive in its summaries.

Before moving, compressing or removing content, use the [preservation check](memory-layout.md#preserving-content-when-reorganizing). Account for distinct constraints, exceptions, rejection reasons, uncertainty and source words. Preserve authorised originals in their existing owner; summaries do not replace them. Historical retention remains subject to the space's withdrawal/deletion authority. File splitting and byte compression alone do not prove reduced reading cost. Do not create access counters, a migration ledger or a mandatory three-folder layout.

## Reconcile changed guidance

When a durable change replaces existing guidance, follow its affected uses within authorised scope. Search routed owners, context entries, agent instructions and relevant maintained summaries, templates or examples for the old names, values and behaviour. Check meaning: renamed mechanisms can still repeat a rejected choice. Search broadly enough to account for affected uses, rather than scanning every source for every task.

Update the current owner in place and reconcile its affected live uses. Fix moved-file and heading links. In historical decisions, retain what was said and why; mark the specific replaced clauses with their successor, keeping unaffected acceptance and exceptions. A later record alone is insufficient when the old entry still appears operative.

For a frozen or unauthorised dependent owner, leave its content unchanged. In the nearest authorised route or existing follow-up owner, identify the affected topic or obsolete concept, the current owner section and the established reopening condition (or say it is unknown). Make the affected part recognisable without copying the entire replacement. Replace obsolete intermediate follow-ups rather than accumulating another full copy of the target facts. Treat the affected content as pending, not current; unrelated valid content and independent work remain usable. Unlocking requires the applicable authority; once granted, refresh from the current owner, reconcile the pending uses and clear only completed deferrals.

Before calling this change reconciled, read back the changed owner, affected live uses, historical replacement notices and any pending boundary. Report unresolved affected uses; keyword or link checks cannot establish semantic agreement. For a tiering change, also try a representative task from the entry and a targeted historical question. Compare whole-task reading, retrieval, writing and mistakes; count maintenance cost and preserve important exceptions. Without observed task evidence, claim only the structural changes checked.
