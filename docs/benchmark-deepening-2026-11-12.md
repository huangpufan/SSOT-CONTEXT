## State model and coverage denominator

Relevant transitions are: authorized task → discover entry/skills → read current owner → capture accepted scope → scoped correction → writer exits → transfer only declared documents → fresh consumer acts → independent executable judgment → artifact-bound semantic judgment → report → durable archive/readback. An attempt may instead stop at an authority violation, incomplete execution, unknown model, broken observation or infrastructure failure. A later clean state cannot erase a previous observed violation. Missing receipts cannot become a successful task.

## Baseline finding and measurement repairs

B0 completed seven Sol turns: one reporting ceiling, two plain writer turns plus a consumer, and two protocol writer turns plus a consumer. Ceiling and baseline were reviewed against their exact artifacts. Plain gave plans without persisting either accepted decision; its fresh consumer correctly reported missing policy. These automatic-capture prompts asked for a plan, not an explicit save. This observes an unusable cold handoff under that treatment, not refusal of an explicit persistence request or poor ordinary-document quality. Controls were incomplete, so B0 has no eligible cost comparison. Its during-run report also misclassified `started` as observation failure; that historical report remains retained.

The independent Astra review found three measurement defects against the fixed baseline: reporting misclassified an in-flight receipt and could abort on corrupt JSON; a forbidden token in `.git` escaped the task privacy check; medium reasoning was requested but native effort was not verified. These are benchmark defects, not evidence for more SSOT protocol duties. B1 preserves started state, isolates damaged attempts while recovering known phase spend, scans hidden project files without following links and treats scan gaps as observation failure, and verifies actual model plus reasoning before comparison. Unknown or wrong identity never erases known spend. Forty-eight offline benchmark tests cover valid, mutated and alternate accepted outcomes, including the new boundary checks.

Real completed B0 artifacts were copied into twelve calibration samples: valid, missing result, corrupt result, missing review, corrupt review and started, each reported with B0 and B1. Original evidence hashes were checked unchanged. B0 silently treated a missing result as started with unknown spend, aborted the whole report on corrupt result/review, and misclassified started as observation failure. B1 retained a valid pass, rejected missing/corrupt sealed evidence only for the affected row, preserved the other native attempts and known spend, and kept the unsealed receipt started. Missing review was rejected in both. This calibrates the actual observed evidence path; no native receipt or subject output was regenerated.

## Observed handoff outcomes and decision

All eighteen B1 handoff native phases completed with actual `gpt-6.1-sol`, medium reasoning, valid per-turn usage and complete observation. Both domains’ ceiling, absent-decision and stale-decision controls behaved as specified and received artifact-bound executor review; a separate Astra review checked all eighteen phase identities/usage and ten sealed attempts. Both explicit writers in each domain left usable current knowledge, accepted scope/history and honest completion claims, and their fresh consumers produced the correct deliverables. Museum plain and protocol both retained the explicitly required necessary original words, including the boundary against donor contact or collection-record changes. Protocol reporting also retained original words; reporting plain’s legitimate paraphrase meets its different task contract.

Reporting plain’s first owner uses the phrase “scoped correction” imprecisely, but the same snapshot says the user initially accepted the policy, replaces an unsettled owner and explicitly asserts no earlier accepted format. The executor, coordinator and separate Astra review judged this in its first-turn context: it does not invent prior accepted history. That is a contextual semantic decision, not a claim of perfect prose, and does not rely on its later corrected owner to erase an earlier violation.

Adopt the benchmark repairs and retain SSOT S0 = 5.3. The observed failures belong to reporting, hidden-file privacy observation and native identity admission. Once the persistence task is explicit, both writer treatments preserve usable knowledge in these bounded cases; no new SSOT duty or protocol candidate is justified by this cycle. This is the feedback decision, rather than an untested protocol wording change.
